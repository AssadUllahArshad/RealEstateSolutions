Transfer update 2026-11-09: an approved transfer carries the remaining installment schedule to the new purchaser while preserving previous payments and ownership history. Owner statement update 2026-11-09: printable statements default to full history with optional dates, show pending payouts separately without reducing official payable, and mask payment references to their final four characters.

## Technical proposals for review

- DECIMAL(18,2) PKR persistence and decimal strings over JSON; BCMath or a verified decimal library in PHP. Percentage intermediates retain documented higher precision; the business rounding mode and residual rule remain D05. Reject overflow and unsupported precision rather than silently truncate.
- Contract amount = accepted price − approved discount/rebate + approved charges. Which charges and taxes participate in owner/commission bases remains D06/D10/D11.
- Receipt available = eligible posted receipt amount − effective allocations − approved returned/unavailable amounts, according to D07/D08. Installment open = effective scheduled charge − effective allocations/adjustments. Do not clamp negative errors away.
- Owner outstanding = eligible entitlement events − settled allocations + linked corrections. Commission outstanding uses its own earned/payable events. A generic outgoing cash row is not an entitlement calculation.
- Reversal preserves original, appends compensation and reverses dependent allocations/movements in one transaction. Refund then references the approved resulting liability; prevent double compensation. Partial reversal semantics require D07/D08.
- Missing rule version prevents affected posting with an actionable configuration error; it never falls back to prototype rates or dates.
