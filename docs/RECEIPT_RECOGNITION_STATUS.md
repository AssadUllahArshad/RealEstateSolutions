## Delivered behavior and explicit choices

This increment implements the next receipt-posting slice following U3's payment-method, assigned-role and admin-finalized correction directions. The role implementation permits accountant/admin/super_admin receipt verification with explicit `finance.receipts.post`; full accounting reversal additionally requires admin/super_admin and `finance.receipts.reverse`. Agents prepare and view assigned-customer reports but cannot post through a misgranted ability alone. No routine admin approval is required for an accountant's verified receipt. These are technical role grants, not newly approved monetary defaults.

Main admin must explicitly save all **Receipt posting controls**: official prefix, first number for a new prefix, minimum number digits, first open posting date, maximum backdating days, permission for unallocated receipts, destination routing and duplicate-reference scope. All start unset. Receiving-payment settings must also be complete. Acceptance before contracts/allocation is available additionally requires both `unallocated = allow` and receiving-payment overpayment `unallocated`. Choosing block/reject leaves acceptance disabled for this unsupported allocation path; it never silently creates an allocation.

Routing is either explicitly selected matching types (cash→cash, transfer/cheque→bank, wallet→wallet, other→other), or an explicit staff-verified any-active-account rule. Duplicate scope is an explicit same-account/method or all-accounts/method choice. Comparison uses the exact external reference after trimming outer spaces, without changing case or punctuation. Staff must still check evidence and differently formatted references; the system does not authenticate bank data. Cash may lack an external reference and is protected against command/source replay, not assumed unique by reference.

Configured dates are Karachi business dates. Future posting is unsupported, and acceptance cannot precede the reported payment date. Open-period and backdating limits are checked again on clearance and reversal. A later event cannot precede any existing acceptance, recognition or clearance date. Main-admin policy changes are versioned; changes while a form is open conflict instead of silently changing the reviewed rules.

## Workflows and financial meaning

1. **Prepare:** existing unposted reports remain editable and are never treated as money. Unsaved preparation edits disable the separate verification form. Verification displays the saved amount and requires explicit date, evidence context and attestation.
2. **Verify and issue:** authorized staff selects the actual cheque stage, or confirms the non-cheque payment, and receives a configured official number. The source preparation freezes permanently, including if the receipt is later reversed. Reopening its old edit URL redirects to the issued receipt. Amount, customer, method, destination, original reference and policy snapshots are immutable through the application model.
3. **Recognize:** verified cash/transfer/wallet/other creates one immutable receipt credit. A cheque recognized on bank clearance remains `pending_clearance` after deposit and creates no movement until clearance. A cheque configured for deposit recognition creates its credit on verified deposit but still displays **not bank-cleared**. Marking that cheque cleared does not credit it twice. The trigger captured at acceptance is retained; subsequent settings do not rewrite old receipts.
4. **Accounting reversal:** authorized admin records a reason, date and explicit confirmation. The original number/history remains, state becomes `reversed`, and any receipt credit receives one equal offsetting receipt-reversal movement. Reversing an uncleared, unrecognized instrument creates no artificial cash movement. This is an accounting correction, **not cash-refund execution** or cancellation of a booking. Reversal is blocked while principal allocations remain; an otherwise reversible receipt automatically receives compensating reversals for its manual-charge allocations.

Recognized funds begin as **unallocated customer credit**. Final booking and later installment allocation consume eligible credit under shared locks; principal is allocated before manual charges. Only principal allocations accrue owner/commission entitlement or become refundable principal. A recognized-on-deposit cheque is not silently represented as bank-cleared.

## Numbering, immutability and concurrency

The additive [migration](../database/migrations/2026_11_04_000400_create_receipt_recognition.php) creates `receipt_series`, `payment_receipts`, `account_movements`, `payment_receipt_events` and `receipt_posting_commands`. Restrictive FKs, unique source preparation, unique official number/prefix-sequence, unique receipt/movement kind, unique actor/command key, unique receipt/revision and MySQL positive/state checks apply. Money is DECIMAL(18,2), exposed as decimal strings. The receipt ledger is operational incoming-money evidence, not optional double-entry GL.

The [posting action](../app/Domain/Finance/ReceiptPosting.php) rechecks a fresh actor, serializes the installation for sequence/reference/policy changes, locks the currently accessible customer/account and policy/source rows, and commits number allocation, receipt, movement, audit and command together. A failed audit consumes no committed number or money. The installation lock deliberately prioritizes correctness over high-volume parallel posting for one office. Current locking reads are used for reference/source checks; a pre-lock repeatable-read snapshot cannot authorize a duplicate after another writer commits.

Each successful command stores an actor-scoped UUID and keyed payload hash. Same-payload replay returns the same currently accessible receipt without repeating its event or movement. Changed payload under the key conflicts. Different commands accepting the same preparation cannot produce a second receipt. Different clearances/reversals must carry the current version; stale content cannot execute after a conflicting state change. Three deadlock retries are bounded. No exception path substitutes a second number or silently treats a unique conflict as success.

Each prefix has a permanent sequence initialized only from its explicit starting number. It advances atomically at successful issue and stops above 999999999. Prefix reuse cannot reset its starting number, even after reversal; settings validation and posting both enforce it. Padding is the configured minimum width. New prefixes are separate explicit sequences. No reset, deletion, posted-amount edit or paper-number workaround is provided.

Non-cash duplicate fingerprints are HMAC values, with same-account hashes unique and global hashes indexed for the configured broader check. The application keyring is checked on replay and duplicate lookup to support rotation. Keep prior keys while encrypted history/fingerprints still depend on them. A reversed reference remains reserved; legitimate reissue needs a future explicit linked correction workflow. Staff must not alter a reference to bypass that protection.

Issued receipt terms, movement rows and event rows are immutable at the application-model layer. This is not protection against a privileged database administrator; restricted DB credentials, encrypted backup/key retention and audited operational access remain live-release requirements.

## UI, privacy and reporting

The [verification panel](../resources/js/components/receipt-posting.tsx), [issued-receipt screen](../resources/js/pages/finance/posted-receipt.tsx) and [movement view](../resources/js/pages/finance/movements.tsx) distinguish preparation, pending clearance, recognized funds and reversal with text and dates. Independent form keys include source versions so stale form state cannot survive a revised preparation; clear and reversal keys cannot collide. Required attestations are unchecked, and dates/stages are not guessed. Customer receipt lists show the issued number/state after acceptance, with mobile cards and desktop tables.

Financial account movements deliberately omit opening balances, expenses and other movements. They must **not** be presented as complete account balances, a cashbook or a formal balance sheet. No zero opening balance is inferred. Source account and policy snapshots are retained along with customer name at acceptance. External reference, verification context and event reasons are encrypted at rest; raw fingerprints and verification note are hidden from ordinary model serialization. Only scoped receipt details expose the external reference; private action reasons are shown only to authorized posting/reversal staff. Agents retain current-assignment receipt visibility without accountant access to CRM identity documents. HTTPS and encrypted Inertia history remain applicable.
